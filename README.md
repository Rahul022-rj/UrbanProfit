# UrbanProfit: Market Potential Prediction & Visualization

## Project Overview
UrbanProfit is a data analytics and machine learning project designed to evaluate the market potential of different business categories across Bengaluru.

## City
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

## Business Categories
1. Cafe / Restaurant
2. Retail Shop
3. Gym / Fitness Center
4. Pharmacy
5. Salon / Beauty Center
6. Electronics Store

## Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Scikit-learn
- Plotly
- GeoPandas and OSMnx
- Folium
- Streamlit

## Project Objectives
- Collect Bengaluru business location data.
- Analyze population and commercial distribution.
- Identify business competition and location patterns.
- Develop market potential prediction models.
- Build an interactive geospatial analytics dashboard.
